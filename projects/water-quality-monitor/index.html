Undergraduate project · Final-year academic project · Data acquisition

Portable Water-Quality Monitoring.

A portable prototype developed to collect pH, turbidity, total dissolved solids, and temperature measurements.

Abubakar Resume ↓

The problem

Obtaining measurements is only the first step. The next challenge is determining how reliably they describe changing conditions.

What I explored or implemented

  • Connected sensing components and developed a reading workflow.
  • Worked with measured values from multiple environmental variables.
  • Observed practical issues related to sensor values, data quality, and debugging.

Outcome and current status

An academic hardware/software project that shaped subsequent interest in data quality and confidence-aware computational systems.

Tools and context
Data collectionSensor readingsEmbedded prototypingDebugging
