Learning prototype · Python · Google Colab · Applied machine learning

AquaSense AI.

An exploratory Google Colab notebook for working with water-quality data. The project studies preprocessing, missing observations, and basic modeling and evaluation workflows, with emphasis on understanding what the data does and does not support.

Open Google Colab notebook ↗Abubakar Resume ↓

The problem

Environmental sensor datasets can include gaps, inconsistent measurements, and limited coverage. These issues can weaken predictions and create false confidence.

What I explored or implemented

  • Inspect the structure of water-quality observations and identify incomplete fields.
  • Prepare tabular inputs in Python and explore numerical distributions.
  • Experiment with baseline modeling and evaluation steps as part of Python/ML learning.
  • Document limitations, including missing readings, generalization, and the need for independent validation.

Outcome and current status

A notebook-based learning prototype, not a validated water-quality safety classifier or a published research result.
