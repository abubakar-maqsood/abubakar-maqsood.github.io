01 / Proposed research direction

Evidence-aware uncertainty in language models.

A working research concept for future Master's study. It is a starting point for discussion with prospective supervisors, not a completed proposal, publication, or validated novel contribution.

01
THE RESEARCH QUESTION

Can an LLM-assisted system recognize when the available evidence is insufficient or contradictory, and make a better decision about whether to answer, verify, or abstain?

Language models may generate confident-sounding answers even when their supporting material is incomplete, outdated, or conflicting. I want to study this failure mode through systematic experiments rather than assume that a better prompt alone makes responses trustworthy.

Why it mattersReliability is especially important when someone uses an AI answer for a real decision. A useful system should communicate uncertainty and trace its answer back to supporting evidence.
